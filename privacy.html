Ultimate Dashboard Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy describes how Ultimate Dashboard handles information for the optional daily task reminder SMS program.

Information and use

If you opt in, Ultimate Dashboard stores your mobile number, selected time zone, and consent status to provide and manage your reminders. Task titles and categories from your dashboard may be included in the reminders. Your number and consent information are used to operate the service, respond to your settings, and honor opt-outs.

Text message program

If you opt in on the text reminders page, you will receive recurring automated daily task reminder texts from Ultimate Dashboard. Message frequency: up to 1 message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Sharing of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Your choices

You may pause reminders in your dashboard or reply STOP to a reminder. Pausing the program prevents future reminder messages until you resume them on the text reminders page. Consent is recorded once for each mobile number; entering a different number requires agreeing again.

For the program's schedule, rates, and other messaging details, read the Ultimate Dashboard SMS Terms.
